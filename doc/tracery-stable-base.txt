Stable-base stained-glass editing

TraceryDocument owns an immutable mesh and material/selection polygons in the
coordinate frame of each base facet. Every successful edit rebuilds its derived
mesh from that base. The derived-to-base facet table transports brush changes
and ordinary paint/support/seam changes; derived triangle IDs are never used as
the source of a later geometric subdivision.

The eight channels are final MMU paint, support, seam, fuzzy skin, exterior,
underlying ordinary paint, selection mask and committed selection mask.
Erase restores underlying paint only within the erased area. Other material
regions retain their polygons and colours. Adjacent same-colour regions may be
merged: this document preserves coloured regions, not separate Voronoi seed IDs.
The gizmo's fragment/history views are derived display/editing caches.

Rendering and slicing consume one mesh with normal FacetsAnnotation paint.
The persistent document is not a second rendered surface. It is copied with the
volume, included in undo snapshots and saved as tracery_edit_data_v1 metadata in
BBS project 3MF files. Exporting plain STL or generic geometry 3MF does not retain
editable stained-glass metadata. Mesh replacement, geometry recentering/scaling
and resetting annotations invalidate it. Instance transforms do not alter the
local base. Material removal/reindexing also remaps hidden underlying colours.
Legacy projects without this document capture their current mesh as the base;
geometry already subdivided by an older build cannot be reconstructed backwards.

Only a successfully validated result is committed. Zero-area faces, duplicate
faces, new unmatched/non-manifold edges and inconsistent internal edge winding
are rejected. Existing source boundaries are allowed. Shared source-edge cuts
are canonical; float-degenerate connected edges and collapsed boundary chains
are reconciled without welding unrelated coincident shells. Facets narrower
than the existing 0.00001 mm geometry tolerance keep their dominant channel
state instead of inserting unrepresentable internal colour cuts. Their shared
edge cuts and authoritative editing polygons remain available. Source mesh winding
errors are not silently repaired by this feature.

Regression coverage: tracery_document and tracery_partition, including repeated
paint/erase, ordinary paint/support preservation, material-slot changes, real
project save/reopen, sub-micron source facets and the sanitized window fixture.
The window fixture contains geometry and paint only, with no printer presets or
user paths. Brush tests exercise the production circle selector, partition,
serialization and erase at radii 1, 3 and 8 mm; they do not simulate GUI events.
